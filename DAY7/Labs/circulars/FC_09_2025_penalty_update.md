# Circular FC/09/2025: Revised Late Payment Penalty (Training Edition)

**Date issued:** 15 September 2025
**Effective:** 1 October 2025
**To:** All contributing employers
**Supersedes:** paragraph 1 of FC/07/2024

1. With effect from 1 October 2025, the penalty for late payment of contributions is **reduced to 2.5% of
   the unpaid contribution for each month or part of a month** of delay.
2. The penalty is capped at **50% of the unpaid contribution**.
3. Penalties already charged for months before October 2025 are not recalculated.
4. The one-time amnesty in FC/07/2024 has ended and has not been renewed.
