# 4. Old Age Pension

## 4.1 Retirement age
- **Compulsory retirement age:** 60 years.
- **Early (voluntary) retirement:** from 55 years, if you stop working.

## 4.2 Who qualifies for a pension
You qualify for a monthly old age pension if you have at least **180 months (15 years) of contributions**
when you retire.

If you have fewer than 180 months of contributions at retirement, you do not receive a monthly pension.
Instead you receive an **old age grant**: a single payment equal to your total contributions plus interest.

## 4.3 How the pension is calculated
1. Work out your **average pensionable earnings (APE)**: the average of your best 5 years of annual wages
   out of the last 10 years before retirement.
2. **Annual pension = (number of contribution months ÷ 580) × APE.**
3. The annual pension cannot be more than 72.5% of APE.

Example: if you have 300 contribution months and an APE of TZS 12,000,000:
annual pension = 300 ÷ 580 × 12,000,000 = TZS 6,206,897, which is about TZS 517,241 per month before
commutation.

## 4.4 Lump sum (commutation)
When you retire you receive a **lump sum of 33%** of your pension commuted using a factor of 12.5. The
remaining 67% is paid as your monthly pension for life.

- Lump sum = 33% × annual pension × 12.5
- Monthly pension after commutation = 67% × annual pension ÷ 12

## 4.5 Minimum pension
The minimum monthly pension is **TZS 100,000**.

## 4.6 Keeping your pension active: life verification
Every pensioner must confirm that they are alive **once every 12 months**. This is called life
verification. You may verify at any branch with your NIDA card. If you do not verify within 12 months, your
pension is **suspended** until you verify. It is not cancelled, and arrears are paid once you verify.

*Note: circular FC/03/2025 changes how life verification is done.*

## 4.7 Documents for an old age pension claim
- Completed claim form
- Copy of NIDA card
- Letter from your last employer confirming your date of retirement
- Bank account details in your own name (bank letter or bank statement)
- Two recent passport-size photographs
