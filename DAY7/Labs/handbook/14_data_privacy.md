# 14. Your Personal Data

The Fund collects and uses your personal data to register you, record contributions and pay benefits. The
Fund handles personal data in line with the **Personal Data Protection Act, 2022**.

- Your data is used only for social security purposes, or where the law requires it to be shared.
- You have the right to see the personal data the Fund holds about you and to ask for corrections.
- The Fund does not sell your data or share it for marketing.
- Staff may access your record only when they need to for their work, and access is logged.
- If you think your data has been misused, complain through the channels in Section 12.
