# 5. Invalidity Benefit

## 5.1 Who qualifies
You may receive an invalidity pension if the Fund's **Medical Board** assesses that you are permanently
unable to work because of illness or injury that is not related to your work, and you have:
- at least **36 months of contributions**, and
- at least **12 months of contributions in the 36 months** before the invalidity began.

## 5.2 How much you receive
The invalidity pension is calculated in the same way as the old age pension (Section 4.3), but the Fund
adds the months you would have contributed until age 60, up to a maximum of 180 extra months.

## 5.3 Reviews
The Medical Board may review your condition every two years. If you recover and return to work, the
invalidity pension stops.

## 5.4 Documents
- Claim form and copy of NIDA card
- Medical report from a registered hospital, signed by a medical officer
- Letter from your employer confirming the date you stopped working
- Bank account details in your own name
