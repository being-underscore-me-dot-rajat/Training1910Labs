# 9. Employment Injury

The Fund **does not pay employment injury or occupational disease benefits.** Compensation for injuries,
diseases or deaths caused by work is paid by the **Workers Compensation Fund (WCF)**.

If you are injured at work:
1. Get medical treatment and keep all medical reports.
2. Make sure your employer reports the accident to the Workers Compensation Fund.
3. Contact the Workers Compensation Fund directly to claim.

Being injured at work does not affect your membership of this Fund. If the injury leaves you permanently
unable to work, you may *also* qualify for an invalidity benefit from this Fund (Section 5).
