# 10. Unemployment Benefit

## 10.1 Who qualifies
You may receive unemployment benefit if you **lose your job** and you:
- have at least **18 months of contributions**,
- are under **55 years** of age, and
- lost your job because of termination, redundancy, or the end of a fixed-term contract.

You do **not** qualify if you resigned voluntarily or were dismissed for gross misconduct.

## 10.2 How much is paid
**33.3% of your average monthly wage over the last 6 months**, paid monthly for up to **6 months**.

## 10.3 When the benefit stops
Payments stop as soon as you start a new job. You must tell the Fund within 14 days of starting new
employment. Payments received after you start work must be refunded.

## 10.4 Documents and time limit
- Claim form and copy of NIDA card
- Termination letter, redundancy letter or copy of the expired contract
- Certificate of service from the employer
- Claim within **90 days** of losing your job

## 10.5 Effect on your pension
Months in which you receive unemployment benefit are **not** counted as contribution months.
