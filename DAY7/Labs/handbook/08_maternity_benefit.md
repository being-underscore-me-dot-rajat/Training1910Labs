# 8. Maternity Benefit

## 8.1 Who qualifies
A female member qualifies if she has:
- at least **36 months of contributions**, and
- at least **6 months of contributions in the 12 months** before the expected date of delivery.

## 8.2 How much is paid
The maternity benefit is **100% of the average monthly wage over the last 6 months**, paid for
**12 weeks (84 days)**.

## 8.3 Limits
The benefit is paid for a maximum of **four confinements**, with at least **three years** between each
claim. If the baby is stillborn or dies, the benefit is still paid.

## 8.4 Documents and time limit
- Claim form and copy of NIDA card
- Birth notification or clinic card showing the date of delivery
- Letter from the employer confirming maternity leave
- Claim within **6 months** of delivery
