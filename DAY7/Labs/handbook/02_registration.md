# 2. Registration

## 2.1 Registering an employer
Every employer with at least one employee must register with the Fund within **30 days** of employing its
first worker. The employer receives an employer registration number, which must appear on every
contribution schedule it submits.

Documents needed to register an employer:
- Certificate of incorporation or business registration
- Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) certificate
- Business licence
- A list of employees with their National Identification (NIDA) numbers

## 2.2 Registering an employee
An employer must register each new employee within **30 days** of the date the employee starts work. The
employee receives a member number. **The member number is yours for life**: if you change employers, you
keep the same number. Do not register again with a new employer; ask the new employer to use your existing
member number.

To register, the Fund needs your NIDA number, full name, date of birth, phone number, and the names of your
dependants (spouse and children).

## 2.3 Voluntary and self-employed members
If you are self-employed or work in the informal sector (for example farming, fishing, small trading or
transport), you can join the Fund as a voluntary member. The minimum voluntary contribution is
**TZS 20,000 per month**. You may pay monthly, quarterly or annually, using mobile money or a bank.

## 2.4 Updating your details
Tell the Fund within 30 days when your phone number, bank account, marital status or dependants change.
Changes to bank details must be made in person at a branch or through the member portal after identity
verification. **For your protection, the Fund does not accept changes to bank details by phone call,
SMS or email.**
