# Circular FC/03/2025: Digital Life Verification for Pensioners (Training Edition)

**Date issued:** 3 March 2025
**Effective:** 1 April 2025
**To:** All pensioners and branch staff

1. From 1 April 2025, pensioners may complete their annual life verification **through the mobile app** using
   NIDA fingerprint or face verification, in addition to verifying at a branch.
2. Pensioners aged **75 years and above**, or who are bedridden, may request a **home visit** by branch staff
   by calling the call centre.
3. The verification period stays at **12 months**. A pension is suspended, not cancelled, when verification is
   overdue.
4. Branch staff must not accept life verification through a third party who is not registered as the
   pensioner's guardian.
