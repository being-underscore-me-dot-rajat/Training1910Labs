# 11. How to Make a Claim

## 11.1 Where to claim
Claims can be lodged at any branch or through the member portal. When you claim online, upload clear copies
of every document. Original documents may be requested before payment.

## 11.2 What happens after you claim
1. **Acknowledgement:** you receive an SMS with a claim reference number.
2. **Verification:** the Fund checks your contribution record, your documents and your eligibility.
3. **Approval:** a claims officer approves the claim, and a second officer checks the calculation.
4. **Payment:** payment is made to the bank or mobile money account registered in **your own name**.

## 11.3 How long it takes
The Fund's service standard is to pay a **complete** claim within **30 days**. A claim is complete when
all required documents have been received and verified. Claims with missing documents are put on hold, and
you receive an SMS listing what is missing.

## 11.4 Checking the status of your claim
Use your claim reference number on the member portal, the mobile app or the USSD service, or call the call
centre.

## 11.5 Paying benefits to third parties
Benefits are paid only to the member or to the verified dependant. The Fund **never** pays benefits into the
account of an agent, an employer or any other third party, even with a signed letter. If anyone offers to
"speed up" your claim for a fee, report it to the Fund's fraud hotline.
