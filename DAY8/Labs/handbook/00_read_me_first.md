# Member Handbook (Training Edition)

This handbook was written for the Enterprise AI & LLM Engineering course. It is modelled on the kind of
member handbook a social security fund publishes, but it is **not** an official NSSF document.

Some figures are close to current practice and some have been simplified or changed on purpose so that the
labs have clear, testable answers. Never use this handbook to answer a real member's question. When you
are unsure, check the current Act, regulations and official circulars.

Edition: Training Edition 2.1, prepared for course use, 2026.
