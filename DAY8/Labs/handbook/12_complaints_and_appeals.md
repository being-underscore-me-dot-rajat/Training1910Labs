# 12. Complaints and Appeals

## 12.1 How to complain
You can complain at any branch, through the call centre, by email, through the member portal, or through the
Fund's official social media pages. Give your member number and, if there is one, your claim reference
number. Never share your PIN or password.

## 12.2 Service standards for complaints
- Your complaint is **acknowledged within 2 working days**.
- Most complaints are **resolved within 14 working days**.
- If the complaint needs information from your employer, it may take up to 30 working days. You will be told
  if this is the case.

## 12.3 Escalation
If you are not satisfied with the response:
1. Ask for your complaint to be escalated to the **Branch Manager**.
2. If still not satisfied, write to the Fund's **Member Relations Office** at head office.
3. For decisions about benefits, you may lodge a formal **appeal within 60 days** of the decision. Appeals
   are heard by the Fund's Appeals Committee.
