# 13. Digital Services

## 13.1 Member portal and mobile app
Register for the member portal and the mobile app using your member number, NIDA number and phone number. You
can view statements, update some personal details, lodge claims and track claims.

## 13.2 USSD service
The USSD service works on any phone without internet. You can check your balance of contribution months, the
last contribution posted, and the status of a claim. The USSD code is published on the Fund's official
website and in branches.

## 13.3 SMS alerts
The Fund sends SMS alerts when contributions are posted, when a claim is received, when documents are
missing and when a payment is made. **The Fund will never ask you for your PIN, password or a payment by
SMS.**

## 13.4 Forgotten password or PIN
Use "Forgot password" on the portal. A one-time code is sent to your registered phone number. If your
phone number has changed, visit a branch with your NIDA card to update it.
