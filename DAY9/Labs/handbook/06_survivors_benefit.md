# 6. Survivors' Benefit

## 6.1 When it is paid
When a member or pensioner dies, the Fund pays a survivors' pension to the member's dependants if the member
had at least **36 months of contributions**, or was already receiving a pension.

## 6.2 Who counts as a dependant
- Spouse (husband or wife), including a spouse in a marriage recognised under customary or religious law
- Children under 18 years, or under 21 years if in full-time education, or of any age if disabled
- Parents who depended on the member, **only if there is no spouse or child**

## 6.3 How the pension is shared
The survivors' pension is equal to 100% of the pension the member was receiving or would have received.
- Spouse(s): **40%**, shared equally if there is more than one spouse
- Children: **60%**, shared equally
- If there is no spouse, the children share 100%. If there are no children, the spouse(s) receive 100%.
- Parents, when they qualify, share 20% of the member's pension.

## 6.4 Documents
- Claim form
- Death certificate
- Letter of administration or a letter from the local government office naming the dependants
- Marriage certificate (for spouses) and birth certificates (for children)
- Bank account details of each adult dependant, or of the guardian of child dependants

## 6.5 Time limit
Claims should be made within **12 months** of the death. Late claims are considered if there is a good
reason for the delay.
