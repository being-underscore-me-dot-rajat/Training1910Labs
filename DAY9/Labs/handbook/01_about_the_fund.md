# 1. About the Fund

## 1.1 Who the Fund serves
The Fund provides social security to workers in the private sector and in the informal sector on the
Tanzanian mainland. Employees in the public service are covered by a separate public service scheme and are
not members of this Fund. Workers in Zanzibar are covered by the Zanzibar scheme.

## 1.2 What social security means for you
Social security protects you and your family when you lose income because of old age, invalidity, death,
maternity or loss of employment. Your benefits depend on how long you have contributed and on your wages,
so it is in your interest to make sure every month of your contributions is recorded correctly.

## 1.3 Benefits at a glance
| Benefit | Short description | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Old age pension | Monthly pension and a lump sum when you retire | 4 |
| Invalidity benefit | Pension if you become permanently unable to work | 5 |
| Survivors' benefit | Pension to dependants when a member dies | 6 |
| Funeral grant | One-off payment towards funeral costs | 7 |
| Maternity benefit | Cash benefit during maternity leave | 8 |
| Unemployment benefit | Temporary income after loss of employment | 10 |

Employment injury is not paid by the Fund. See Section 9.

## 1.4 Branches and contact
The Fund has branches in every region of the mainland. Opening hours are 08:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday,
except public holidays. You can also reach the Fund through the member portal, the mobile app, the USSD
service and the call centre (see Section 13).
