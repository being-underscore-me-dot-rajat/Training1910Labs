# 7. Funeral Grant

When a member, a pensioner, or the spouse or child (under 18) of a member dies, the Fund pays a **funeral
grant of TZS 500,000** to the person who paid for the funeral.

- The member must have at least 6 months of contributions, or be a pensioner.
- Claim within **6 months** of the death.
- Documents: claim form, death certificate or burial permit, NIDA card of the claimant, and proof of the
  relationship to the deceased.
- Only one funeral grant is paid for each death.
