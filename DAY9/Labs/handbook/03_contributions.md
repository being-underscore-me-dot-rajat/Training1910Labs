# 3. Contributions

## 3.1 Contribution rate
The total contribution is **20% of the employee's gross monthly wage**. The employer pays 10% and deducts
10% from the employee's wage. An employer may choose to pay a larger share, but the employee's share may
never exceed 10%.

Example: if your gross monthly wage is TZS 800,000, the total monthly contribution is TZS 160,000
(TZS 80,000 from your employer and TZS 80,000 deducted from your wage).

## 3.2 When contributions are due
Contributions for a month must reach the Fund **by the last day of the following month**. For example,
contributions for March are due by 30 April. The employer must also submit a contribution schedule listing
each employee, their member number and their gross wage for the month.

## 3.3 Late payment
If contributions are paid late, the employer is charged a **penalty of 5% of the unpaid amount for each
month or part of a month** that the payment is late. Penalties are charged to the employer only. An
employer may not deduct penalties from employees' wages.

*Note: circular FC/07/2024 and circular FC/09/2025 update the penalty rules. Always read this section
together with the latest circular.*

## 3.4 Checking your contributions
You will receive an SMS each time a contribution is posted to your account. You can also request a
statement through the member portal, the mobile app, USSD or any branch. **Check your statement at least
twice a year.** If a month is missing, first ask your employer for proof of payment, then report it to the
Fund (see Section 12).

## 3.5 When an employer does not pay
If your employer deducts your share but does not send it to the Fund, report it to the nearest branch. The
Fund's compliance team will follow up with the employer. Your right to benefits for months in which your
wages were deducted is protected, and the Fund recovers the arrears from the employer.
