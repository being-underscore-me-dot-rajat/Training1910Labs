# Circular FC/07/2024: Late Payment Penalty (Training Edition)

**Date issued:** 1 July 2024
**To:** All contributing employers
**Status:** Partly superseded by FC/09/2025

1. With effect from 1 July 2024, the penalty for late payment of contributions remains **5% of the unpaid
   contribution for each month or part of a month** of delay.
2. Employers who clear all arrears between 1 July 2024 and 31 December 2024 will have **50% of accumulated
   penalties waived**. This is a one-time amnesty.
3. Penalties may not be recovered from employees' wages.
